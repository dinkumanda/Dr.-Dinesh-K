Profile

An academic biography drawn from documented teaching, research and literary practice across South Asia, the Horn of Africa and the Arabian Peninsula.

Dr. Dinesh K is Assistant Professor in the Department of English at Vignan's University, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. He holds a Ph.D. in English Language Teaching from Anna University, Chennai (2023), where his thesis examined ESL teachers' competence and its impact on second language learning in the tertiary-level classroom.

His scholarly work engages with English language teaching at the tertiary level, teacher competence, communicative language teaching, curriculum design, instructional material development, translation studies and cultural representation in literature. His research considers pedagogical practices across diverse cultural contexts, with comparative attention to language-teaching models in Eritrea, Oman, Libya and India.

Alongside academic research, Dr. Dinesh is an active literary practitioner. His poetry and critical essays have appeared in nationally and internationally recognised platforms, including Indian Literature (Sahitya Akademi, New Delhi) and eMerge Magazine (USA). He has contributed to curriculum development through the design and authorship of instructional materials for undergraduate programmes, including a complete teaching package on Indian Writing in English for the Institute of Correspondence Education, University of Madras.

His academic career spans more than two decades and includes teaching appointments in India, Eritrea, Oman and Libya. He holds an IELTS Train-the-Trainer certificate from the British Council (2012) and an ETS TOEFL certificate (2023). His research and creative interests intersect in the areas of language, culture, pedagogy and poetic expression.
